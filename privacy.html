Privacy Policy for AnimeTab

Last updated: April 27, 2026

Overview

AnimeTab is a Chrome extension that replaces your new tab page with anime-style wallpapers, a live clock, and daily motivational quotes. AnimeTab is designed to be offline-first and does not require an account or backend service.

Data Collection and Use

AnimeTab does not collect personal information, browsing history, login data, or usage analytics. The extension does not transmit data to external servers.

AnimeTab only uses chrome.storage.local to save local preferences on your device.

What Is Stored Locally

AnimeTab stores only the following values:

  • currentWallpaperIndex: Tracks the currently active wallpaper index for smooth rotation.
  • featuredDismissed: Remembers whether you dismissed the featured card.
  • customBackgroundEnabled: Stores whether your uploaded media override is enabled.
  • customBackgroundMime: Stores the file type of your uploaded image, GIF, or video.
  • customBackgroundData: Stores your uploaded media content for local reuse in new tabs.
  • lofiStreamUrl, lofiVolume, lofiMuted: Stores your lo-fi player settings.
  • inviteCount, growthMetrics, unlockedRewards: Stores local invite/reward progress.
  • setupCode: Stores your most recent exported/imported setup code.
  • streakCount, lastOpenDate, focusEndAt, ambientMode: Stores habit and focus preferences.

This data remains on your local Chrome profile and is not shared externally by AnimeTab.

Third-Party Services

AnimeTab does not include analytics SDKs, tracking scripts, ad trackers, or external telemetry. The search feature redirects to Google only when you submit a query in the search bar. Optional lo-fi and ambient streams are loaded only when you choose to play them.

Monetization & Affiliate Links

AnimeTab includes a rotating merchandise recommendation section on the new tab page. This section displays anime-related products from Amazon and other retailers via affiliate links.

Affiliate Disclosure: AnimeTab is a participant in the Amazon Associates Program and other affiliate networks. When you click on a product recommendation and make a purchase, we earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. These commissions help support the continued development of AnimeTab.

Products rotate hourly based on the current time. You can manually rotate to see other products using the "Next" button. Clicking on a product opens a new tab with the affiliate link.

No Personal Data is Shared: Your clicks on affiliate links are not tracked or stored on your device. The affiliate information (product name, price, category) is only used for analytics purposes and is not linked to your personal information.

Analytics

AnimeTab uses Google Analytics to measure feature usage and affiliate link engagement. This helps us understand which features are popular and how to improve the extension.

What We Track:

  • Affiliate product clicks and purchases (product name, price, category only)
  • Manual merch rotations
  • General feature usage patterns

Google Analytics is configured with privacy-first settings: IP anonymization is enabled, and we do not allow Google to personalize ads based on your activity in AnimeTab.

Data Sharing

AnimeTab does not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties. Google Analytics data is subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

Data Retention and Removal

Stored preferences remain in your browser until you clear extension data or uninstall AnimeTab. Uninstalling the extension removes its local stored data from Chrome.
