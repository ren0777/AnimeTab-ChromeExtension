Privacy Policy for AnimeTab

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Overview

AnimeTab is a Chrome extension that replaces your new tab page with anime-style wallpapers, a live clock, greeting, daily quotes, a merch recommendation card, and an optional airing countdown widget. AnimeTab has no accounts, no backend service, and no servers of its own.

Data Collection and Use

AnimeTab does not collect personal information, browsing history, login data, or any usage analytics. It does not transmit your data anywhere. Everything the extension remembers stays in chrome.storage.local (and, for uploaded pictures, the extension's local IndexedDB) on your device.

What Is Stored Locally

AnimeTab stores only the following values on your device:

  • nekosWallpaperUrl / anime_bg_url: the last wallpaper shown, so it can be restored instantly.
  • wallpaperPinnedUrl: the wallpaper you chose to pin.
  • animeThemeMode: your selected color mood.
  • customBackgroundRef, customBackgroundMime, customBackgroundEnabled: pointers to your uploaded background, which is stored in local IndexedDB.
  • quickLinksV1: your customized quick-link tiles.
  • searchEngine: your preferred search engine.
  • clockFormat: 12-hour or 24-hour clock preference.
  • merchRotationState: which merch item you last saw, so rotation stays consistent.
  • animetabMetrics: anonymous feature-use counters (see Analytics below).
  • anilistAiringCache: a local 6-hour cache of public airing schedule data (see Third-Party Services below).

This data remains on your local Chrome profile and is not shared externally by AnimeTab.

Third-Party Services

AnimeTab contacts only these services, always without identifying information:

  • nekos.best — source of random anime wallpapers (public/static image CDN). When offline or blocked, a bundled fallback wallpaper is used instead.
  • graphql.anilist.co (AniList) — public airing schedule data used for the "Airing Soon" card. The response is cached locally for 6 hours to keep requests rare. No account or personal data is involved.
  • Google favicon service — used only if you add or view quick-link tiles, to display a site's favicon image.

AnimeTab never contacts Amazon or any retailer in the background: links open only when you click a product.

Monetization & Affiliate Links

AnimeTab includes a rotating merchandise recommendation card. It displays curated anime-related products from Amazon via affiliate links.

Affiliate Disclosure: AnimeTab is a participant in the Amazon Associates Program. When you click a product recommendation and make a purchase, we earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. These commissions help support the continued development of AnimeTab.

Products rotate hourly; you can press "→" to see the next one. Clicking a product opens Amazon in a new tab. Your clicks are not reported to any server.

Analytics

AnimeTab has no third-party analytics. It keeps a small, anonymous local counter (e.g. how many fresh tabs you opened this week, and how often you rotate or click merch) so YOU can see usage in the Wallpaper Studio. These counters never leave your device, contain no identifiers, and are deleted automatically after 30 days. Uninstalling the extension removes them.

Data Sharing

AnimeTab does not sell, rent, or share your data with anyone. Nothing you create or upload (custom wallpapers, quick links) is ever uploaded to any server by AnimeTab.

Data Retention and Removal

Stored preferences remain in your browser until you clear extension data or uninstall AnimeTab. Uninstalling the extension removes its locally stored data, including uploaded backgrounds in IndexedDB.
