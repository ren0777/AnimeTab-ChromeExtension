Privacy Policy for AnimeTab

Last updated: April 27, 2026

Overview

AnimeTab is a Chrome extension that replaces your new tab page with anime-style wallpapers, a live clock, and daily motivational quotes. AnimeTab is designed to be offline-first and does not require an account or backend service.

Data Collection and Use

AnimeTab does not collect personal information, browsing history, login data, or usage analytics. The extension does not transmit data to external servers.

AnimeTab only uses chrome.storage.local to save local preferences on your device.

What Is Stored Locally

AnimeTab stores only the following values:

  • currentWallpaperIndex: Tracks the currently active wallpaper index for smooth rotation.
  • featuredDismissed: Remembers whether you dismissed the featured card.
  • customBackgroundEnabled: Stores whether your uploaded media override is enabled.
  • customBackgroundMime: Stores the file type of your uploaded image, GIF, or video.
  • customBackgroundData: Stores your uploaded media content for local reuse in new tabs.

This data remains on your local Chrome profile and is not shared externally by AnimeTab.

Third-Party Services

AnimeTab does not include analytics SDKs, tracking scripts, ad trackers, or external telemetry. The search feature redirects to Google only when you submit a query in the search bar.

Data Sharing

AnimeTab does not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties.

Data Retention and Removal

Stored preferences remain in your browser until you clear extension data or uninstall AnimeTab. Uninstalling the extension removes its local stored data from Chrome.
